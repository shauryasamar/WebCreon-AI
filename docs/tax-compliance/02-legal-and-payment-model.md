## 2. Answers to Non-Negotiable Operational & Financial Questions

### Q1: Who is shown as the seller on product and checkout pages?
- **Answer**: The **Merchant** (Store Trade Name and Legal Entity Name).
- **Implementation**: The storefront UI at `RenderPage.tsx` and checkout at `CustomerOrdersPage.tsx` must explicitly display: `"Sold by: [Merchant Legal Name], [Merchant State], GSTIN: [Merchant GSTIN or 'Unregistered']"`.

### Q2: Who issues the customer product invoice?
- **Answer**: The **Merchant**.
- **Implementation**: Invoices generated by the backend (`TaxInvoice` engine) explicitly list the Merchant as the supplier in compliance with Rule 46. The platform (`WebCreon AI`) is cited in the footer solely as the facilitating Electronic Commerce Operator.

### Q3: Who owns and refunds the customer payment?
- **Answer**: 
  - Central Razorpay MID captures customer funds into platform nodal/escrow.
  - Funds are legally held in trust for the merchant subject to fulfillment and platform deduction agreements.
  - Customer refunds are initiated from the central MID, with matching reversals triggered on the merchant's Razorpay Route split.

### Q4: Who contracts with the customer?
- **Answer**: The **Merchant** enters into the contract of sale with the buyer. The Platform is an intermediary providing the electronic network (terms of use and marketplace agreement).

### Q5: Who bears customer chargebacks and payment disputes?
- **Answer**: The **Merchant**.
- **Implementation**: When `payment.dispute.created` is received from Razorpay, the associated order is placed on hold (`payment_status="disputed"`), and merchant escrow is quarantined. If the dispute is lost, the chargeback amount and associated gateway dispute fee are debited against the merchant's ledger balance.

### Q6: Who bears Razorpay payment processing fees (MDR)?
- **Answer**: **Configurable Policy** (Default: **Merchant bears MDR**).
- **Implementation**: Standard gateway fees (approx. 2% + 18% GST) are deducted from the merchant payout calculation unless the merchant is on a premium SaaS subscription where the platform absorbs gateway fees.

### Q7: Who bears Razorpay Route transfer fees?
- **Answer**: **Platform bears Route transfer fees** (treated as internal platform cost of doing business).

### Q8: Does the platform pass fees to the merchant?
- **Answer**: Yes. The net merchant settlement ledger explicitly breaks down:
  $$\text{Net Payout} = \text{Gross Sales} - \text{Platform Fee} - \text{Platform Fee GST} - \text{GST-TCS} - \text{Income-tax TDS} - \text{Gateway MDR}$$

### Q9: Will customer convenience fees be charged?
- **Answer**: **No**. Products are sold at merchant list price + shipping. If an optional convenience fee is introduced in the future, it constitutes platform revenue and must be billed on a separate B2C tax invoice from the platform to the customer with 18% GST.

## 3. Financial Flow & Numerical Example

### Scenario Specification
- **Item**: Premium Leather Handbag
- **HSN Code**: 4202 (Apparel & Accessories)
- **GST Rate**: 18% (Price Inclusive of GST)
- **Customer Selling Price (MRP)**: ₹2,360.00
- **Delivery Charge**: ₹140.00 (Inclusive of 18% GST = ₹118.64 Base + ₹21.36 GST)
- **Total Customer Payment (Gross GMV)**: **₹2,500.00**
- **Seller Location**: Mumbai, Maharashtra (State Code 27)
- **Customer Location**: Bengaluru, Karnataka (State Code 29) -> **Inter-state Transaction (IGST)**
- **Seller Entity**: Private Limited Company (PAN: Verified, GSTIN: Verified)
- **Platform Commission**: 3.00% on Product Taxable Base

### Step-by-Step Numerical Computation

```
1. Customer Transaction Breakdown (Inter-state: IGST):
   - Product Gross: ₹2,360.00
     * Taxable Product Base: ₹2,360 / 1.18 = ₹2,000.00
     * Product IGST (18%): ₹360.00
   - Shipping Gross: ₹140.00
     * Taxable Shipping Base: ₹140 / 1.18 = ₹118.64
     * Shipping IGST (18%): ₹21.36
   -------------------------------------------------------------
   Total Net Taxable Supplies (TCS Base): ₹2,118.64
   Total Customer Tax Invoice Value:      ₹2,500.00

2. Platform Fees & Statutory Deductions Withheld by WebCreon AI:
   a. Platform Marketplace Commission:
      * Base: 3% of Product Taxable Base (₹2,000.00) = ₹60.00
      * 18% GST on Commission (SAC 998313):
        Platform (MH, 27) to Seller (MH, 27) -> CGST 9% (₹5.40) + SGST 9% (₹5.40) = ₹10.80
      * Total Platform Fee Withheld: ₹70.80

   b. Section 52 GST-TCS Withholding:
      * Base: Net Taxable Supplies = ₹2,118.64
      * Inter-state IGST TCS Rate: 0.50%
      * TCS IGST Withheld: ₹2,118.64 * 0.50% = ₹10.59

   c. Section 194-O Income-tax TDS Withholding:
      * Base: Gross Order Value = ₹2,500.00
      * Entity Type: Company with Verified PAN -> Rate: 0.10%
      * TDS Withheld: ₹2,500.00 * 0.10% = ₹2.50

   d. Payment Gateway MDR (Estimated 2% + 18% GST):
      * Base MDR (2% of ₹2,500): ₹50.00
      * GST on MDR (18%): ₹9.00
      * Total Gateway Cost: ₹59.00

3. Final Settlement to Merchant Linked Account via Razorpay Route:
   - Customer Gross:                      ₹2,500.00
   - Less: Platform Fee & 18% GST:       -₹70.80
   - Less: Section 52 GST-TCS:           -₹10.59
   - Less: Section 194-O Income-tax TDS:  -₹2.50
   - Less: Payment Gateway MDR:          -₹59.00
   -------------------------------------------------------------
   Net Merchant Payout (Route Transfer):  ₹2,357.11 (235,711 Paise)
   Amount Held in Central MID Escrow:      ₹142.89 (Retained for Tax & Fees)
```
